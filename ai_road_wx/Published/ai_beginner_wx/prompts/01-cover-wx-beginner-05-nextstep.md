Use medium contrast, normal saturation, balanced visual weight. The warm palette creates an approachable, friendly feel appropriate for beginner-friendly AI education content.

# Font Application
Use clean geometric sans-serif typography for the title text. Modern, minimal letterforms that convey clarity and professionalism. The Chinese characters should be rendered with crisp, legible strokes — NOT hand-lettered or sketchy. The clean font provides a contrast to the hand-drawn decorative elements.

# Composition

## Type Composition (typography)
Title as primary element occupying 40%+ of the visual area. Minimal supporting visuals with strong typographic hierarchy. The title should be prominently centered or slightly offset left with generous breathing room around it.

## Visual Composition
- Main visual: A hand-drawn sketch/whiteboard-style illustration showing three simple pathway icons (a workflow chain link, a document/knowledge base book, and a code bracket/buddy figure) arranged below or around the title as light decorative elements. These represent the "3 directions" from the article.
- Layout: Title dominates the upper-center area. Below or to the right, three small hand-drawn icon clusters form a horizontal row representing the three paths. Background is a warm cream (#FFFAF0) paper-like surface with subtle paper grain texture.
- Decorative: Hand-drawn wavy connection lines between the three pathway icons suggesting interconnection. Small sketchy arrows pointing forward. Simple doodle-style decorations — stars, underlines beneath key words. Organic rounded shapes in warm orange (#ED8936) and golden yellow (#F6AD55) as accent color splashes behind the title text.

## Color Scheme
- Background: Cream (#FFFAF0) with subtle paper grain texture
- Title text: Deep brown (#744210) for readability on cream background
- Accent decorations: Warm orange (#ED8936), golden yellow (#F6AD55)
- Supporting elements: Terracotta (#C05621) for outlines and arrows
- Soft peach (#FED7AA) for subtle background color blocks behind icon clusters

## Rendering Notes (hand-drawn)
- Sketchy, organic, slightly imperfect strokes for all decorative elements
- Variable line weight — thicker at connection points
- Paper grain and subtle surface texture on the background
- Doodles and simple icons with hand-drawn quality
- Wavy connectors and arrows with natural hand tremor visible
- Casual fills with visible brush direction
- Simple layering with overlap for depth

## Type Notes (typography)
- Title as the hero element — large, bold, highly legible
- Minimal supporting visuals — the hand-drawn elements should NOT compete with the title
- Strong visual hierarchy: Title > decorative icons > background texture
- 40-60% breathing room / whitespace

## Palette Notes (warm)
- Friendly, approachable, human-centered feel
- Rounded shapes, organic curves for decorative elements
- Warm gradient-like color transitions in background blocks
- Avoid cold or clinical feeling — keep it warm and inviting
